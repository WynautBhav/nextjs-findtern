# Pricing surfaces — /pricing, the home rate card, /employers plans

Development-only brief for the three places the rate card appears. Product
truth is in PRODUCT.md; visual tokens in DESIGN.md and docs/design_system.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: three prices a reader can compare in one glance. The surface refuses
the category default of badge soup — "Save N%", sparkles, dashed offer boxes —
and lets the struck-through price and one sentence carry the 2026 offer.

OWN-WORLD: white cards on the brand wash, Poppins medium for numbers with
tabular figures, `brand-700` for the one changeable word in a sentence,
`ink-500` for everything derived. No icons inside price blocks. Hairline rules,
not boxes, separate ledger rows.

STORY: an employer sees ₹5,000 per hire on every plan, sees that Espresso is
free through 2026 and why, reads what a month costs at a real schedule, and
either starts hiring or asks a person.

FIRST VIEWPORT (pricing page): hero title, then the three band cards with the
stacked price (old price small above, current price 30px), monthly line under
the paid rates. The calculator follows as a sentence with inline selects on the
left and a four-row ledger on the right; primary action "Start hiring" lives at
the ledger's foot.

FORM: sentence calculator (first choice; a form of segmented controls and a
dark result tile was built, reviewed, and rejected as template work).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
